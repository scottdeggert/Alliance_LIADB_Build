## 1. Purpose

Where an organization sees who has pledged items and who has expressed interest in volunteering, with the contact details needed to follow up. Two independent tables, one per branch. This surface is read-only and it is the only place in the member portal where another person's contact information is displayed.

## 2. Entry and exit

**Arrives from:** MP-04.
**Leaves to:** MP-04 via the back control.

## 3. Data

**Reads:**
- Item side: `item_pledges` joined to `people` (first_name, last_name, email, phone), `item_pledge_lines` joined to `items` (name), and `item_requests` (title), all scoped to the session organization.
- Volunteer side: `volunteer_signups` joined to `people`, `volunteer_signup_roles` joined to `volunteer_roles` (name), and `volunteer_requests` (title), same scope.

**Writes:** Nothing.
**Functions called:** None.
**Never touches:** anything. Read-only.

## 5. Fields

No inputs. Both tables are display only.

### Item donors table columns

`[CAPTURE]` the exact column set, order, and header labels. Expected: donor name, email, phone, the items pledged with quantities, and `[CAPTURE]` whether the request title and pledge date appear.

**The items column renders a computed summary string.** Format: quantity, the literal character `x`, a space, the item name, with entries separated by a comma and a space. For example `3x Blankets, 2x Pillows`.

This string is computed from `item_pledge_lines` at render time. It is not stored anywhere and there is no column to read it from. `[CAPTURE]` the exact format against the live surface, specifically whether there is a space between the quantity and the item name, whether the separator is a comma or something else, and the ordering of entries within the string. This is the highest-risk small detail on the surface, because the output is compared directly against the current system during side-by-side testing and any difference is immediately visible.

`[CAPTURE]` the ordering rule within the string: the order items appear on the request, alphabetical, or the order they were pledged.

### Volunteer table columns

`[CAPTURE]` the exact column set, order, and header labels. Expected: volunteer name, email, phone, notes, and the roles they expressed interest in.

**The roles column renders a comma-separated list of role names**, resolved from `volunteer_signup_roles`. `[CAPTURE]` the exact separator and the ordering rule.

The `notes` field is the free-text availability, experience, and accommodations text a volunteer entered at PB-04. `[CAPTURE]` whether it appears here. It should, since it is the only place an organization can read it, and a volunteer who wrote about an accommodation they need has a reasonable expectation that someone reads it.

## 7. Conditional behavior

| Trigger | Result |
|---|---|
| No item pledges for the organization | Item table renders its empty state |
| No volunteer signups for the organization | Volunteer table renders its empty state |

The two tables evaluate independently. An organization with pledges but no volunteers sees a populated table and an empty state side by side, and that is the common case.

## 9. Empty states

Both empty states are described above and both need capturing independently. A newly approved organization sees both at once, so this is a first-run screen, not an edge case.

## 10. Mobile differences

`[CAPTURE]`. Two wide tables are the least likely thing on this system to survive a narrow viewport unchanged. Capture whether each row becomes a stacked card, whether columns are dropped, and if so which. A dropped column that carries a volunteer's accommodation notes would be a real loss, so capture this one carefully rather than approximating.

## 11. Authorization

Resolve the session's selected organization per MP-02, then scope every query to that `org_id`, server-side. The organization is never read from the URL; there is no organization identifier in this route and none should be added.

This is the surface where a scoping mistake is most costly. It returns names, email addresses, and phone numbers of members of the public. A query that misses its `org_id` filter exposes one organization's supporter list to another, which is the exact exposure named in `Handbook.md` section 5.

Every query on this surface is written with the organization filter and every one is tested with a second organization's session.

## 12. Error paths

| Failure | Rendered result |
|---|---|
| Either query fails | A stated error in place of that table. Never an empty table, which is indistinguishable from having no supporters |
| A pledge line references an item that no longer exists | Render what resolves and note the gap. Do not drop the whole pledge row silently. `[CAPTURE]` whether item removal is even possible; see MP-09 |
| A pledge or signup has no lines or roles | Render the person's row with an empty items or roles cell rather than omitting the person |

The last row matters: a supporter who reached out is a supporter the organization should be able to contact, even if the detail behind their pledge is incomplete.

## 13. Out of scope for this surface

- Editing or deleting any supporter record.
- Marking a pledge fulfilled or received. That is phase two and explicitly excluded in `Handbook.md` section 17. An agent that adds a checkbox here has violated it.
- Contacting a supporter from within the app. Organizations follow up outside the system.
- Any count, total, or aggregate. Reporting is phase two.
- Export.

## 14. Acceptance

- Both tables show only the session organization's supporters, verified against a second organization's session.
- The item summary string matches the captured format exactly, including separator and spacing.
- The roles list matches the captured separator and ordering.
- A person who both pledged and volunteered appears in both tables, resolved from one `people` row.
- Empty states render independently and correctly.
- A failed query renders a stated error, not an empty table.
- No personal data appears for any organization other than the session's, under any request manipulation.
- Column order, headers, and copy match the desktop screenshot. Mobile matches the mobile screenshot.
