## 1. Purpose

Where a member manages who else at their organization has access. A member is selected, their contact details are shown for confirmation, and they can be removed.

This surface edits organization details (mission, website, address, logo, populations) and manages members in one place (D59). Fields for both mirror MP-03 section 5 for the org side and this spec's original section 5 for the member side. Field lists remain `[CAPTURE]` pending the live capture of this specific page; the shape question is closed.

## 2. Entry and exit

**Arrives from:** MP-04.
**Leaves to:** MP-04 via the back control.

## 3. Data

**Reads:** `org_memberships` for the session organization joined to `people` (first_name, last_name, email, phone) and `users` (status).
**Writes:** `org_memberships.status`, set to `removed`.
**Functions called:** None.
**Never touches:** `people`. Removing a member never deletes or modifies the person. It never touches `users` either; the account survives, the access does not.

## 11. Authorization

Verify the session user holds an active membership in the organization, server-side, before reading. Every membership read and the removal write are both filtered by the session organization.

**Any active member may remove another member (D5).** No owner-only restriction. Staff approval at ADMIN-03 is the control that makes open membership safe.

**A member cannot remove themselves (D6).** Prevent self-removal regardless of what the current surface allows; count it as a defect fix rather than a deviation.

## 12. Error paths

| Failure | Rendered result |
|---|---|
| Removal would leave the organization with zero active members | Blocked, with a stated reason. This locks the organization out and staff have to repair it manually |
| The selected member was already removed by someone else | Treat as a no-op success rather than an error. The intent is satisfied |
| Update fails | Failure message. Nothing changes |
| Removed member has an active session | Their next request fails the membership check at MP-02 and they lose access. Verify this |

## 13. Out of scope for this surface

- Inviting members. MP-06.
- Deleting a person or a user account. Removal is a status change. Invariant 7 in `Handbook.md` section 6.
- Approving a pending membership. ADMIN-03.
- Changing a member's role. Not built this week.

## 14. Acceptance

- The selector lists only the session organization's members.
- Selecting a member shows that person's email and phone.
- Removal sets `org_memberships.status` to `removed` and writes nothing to `people` or `users`.
- The removed person's `people` row still exists afterward, with their pledge and signup history intact.
- Removing the last active member is blocked.
- A member cannot remove themselves.
- A removed member with an open session loses access on their next request.
- Field order, labels, and messages match the desktop screenshot. Mobile matches the mobile screenshot.
