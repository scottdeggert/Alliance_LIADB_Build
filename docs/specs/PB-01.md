## 1. Purpose

The public list of item needs. A visitor scans active requests, searches to narrow them, and opens one to donate. This is the top of the donation funnel and most of its traffic arrives from social links on phones, so the mobile layout carries more weight here than anywhere else in the system.

## 2. Entry and exit

**Arrives from:** the site navigation, social and email links, the legacy hub redirect, and PB-02 via back navigation.
**Leaves to:** PB-02 `/items/:id`, by selecting a request card.

An unmatched legacy request identifier also lands here rather than on a 404, per `Handbook.md` section 11.

## 3. Data

**Reads:** `item_requests` where `status = 'active'`, joined to `organizations` where `kind = 'member_org'` and `status = 'approved'`. Fields: request title, description, image, and the organization's name and city.

**Writes:** Nothing.
**Functions called:** None.

**Never returns:** any contact person, any email or phone, any supporter data, any request not `active`, or any organization not `approved`. The public filter is a shared query helper, written once and used by PB-01 through PB-04. It is not re-implemented per surface.

### Card contents

`[CAPTURE]` the exact contents of a request card and their arrangement. Expected: image, request title, organization name, and the organization's city rendered as location.

**Location is the organization's `city`.** It resolves through the request's `org_id` and there is no other source. This is why `city` is required on every approved organization; see `Handbook.md` section 14.

`[CAPTURE]` whether the card shows any additional detail: deadline, item count, remaining totals, or a truncated description. Do not add any of these if they are not present. A count of remaining items would be useful and is not this week's decision.

Card styling tokens are in `Design.md`: 5px radius and the confirmed box shadow. Those values are measured, not estimated; use them rather than eyeballing the screenshot.

## 6. Actions

**Card selection**
- Enabled when: always.
- Does: navigates to PB-02 for that request.
- No write.

**Search input**
- Filters the list as the visitor types, debounced.
- Searches: request title, organization name, and organization city.
- `[CAPTURE]` the debounce interval. Match the feel; time it during capture if a number cannot be read.
- `[CAPTURE]` whether matching is case-insensitive substring or something narrower. Assume case-insensitive substring across all three fields until confirmed.
- No write, no navigation, no page reload.

## 11. Authorization

None. Public by design.

The authorization work on this surface is negative: confirming that nothing beyond the fields in section 3 is serialized into the response. A card that renders the organization's name and city while the underlying payload also carries the request's contact email is a data leak that no visual review catches. Check the response body, not the rendered page.

## 12. Error paths

| Failure | Rendered result |
|---|---|
| Request query fails | A stated error. Never an empty list, which reads as no needs currently posted and quietly turns away a donor |
| A request's organization does not resolve | Exclude that request from the list and log it. Do not render a card with a missing organization name |

## 13. Out of scope for this surface

- Any claim or donation action. That happens on PB-02.
- Filtering by population, city, or organization. Search only.
- Sorting controls.
- Counts, totals, or impact figures of any kind.

## 14. Acceptance

- Only `active` requests belonging to `approved` `member_org` organizations appear. Verify by setting a request to each other status and confirming it disappears.
- Each card shows the correct organization's name and city.
- Search filters on all three fields and is case-insensitive.
- Search debounce matches the captured interval.
- Both empty states render with their captured text.
- The response payload contains no contact details, no email, no phone, and no supporter data.
- A failed query renders a stated error, not an empty list.
- Card shadow and radius match the `Design.md` tokens.
- Desktop and mobile match their screenshots.
