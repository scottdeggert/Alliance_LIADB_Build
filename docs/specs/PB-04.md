## 1. Purpose

Where a member of the public reads a volunteer opportunity and expresses interest in specific roles. No login.

**This surface records interest, not commitment.** A person who checks a role is saying they want to hear more, and the organization follows up. That distinction runs through the whole surface: the counter is `quantity_interested`, the confirmation email sets an expectation of follow-up rather than thanking someone for a completed act, and the organization tracks who actually served in a separate column the public never touches. Do not write copy or logic that treats a checkbox as a booking.

## 2. Entry and exit

**Arrives from:** PB-03, direct links, and the legacy redirect `/area-needs-volunteer-request/:legacyId`.

**Leaves to:** `[CAPTURE]` the post-submit destination and what the visitor is told next.

## 3. Data

**Reads:** `volunteer_requests` for `:id` where `status = 'active'`, `volunteer_roles` for that request, `organizations` (name, mission, website_url, logo_url, city, populations).

**Writes:** entirely through `record_volunteer_signup()`. The surface writes nothing directly. `email_log` rows for the two sends.

**Functions called:** `record_volunteer_signup(first_name, last_name, email, phone, request_id, notes, role_ids)`.

**Never touches:** `volunteer_roles.quantity_interested`, `quantity_remaining`, `quantity_confirmed`, or `volunteer_requests.status`.

`quantity_confirmed` deserves a specific note: it is the organization's private record of who actually served, edited at MP-12. Nothing public ever reads or writes it.

## 4. Layout regions

1. **Request detail.** Title, description, details, image, event location, deadline.
2. **Organization detail.** Name, mission, website, logo, city, populations served.
3. **Roles list.** One row per role, with an interest checkbox.
4. **Volunteer form.** Name, email, phone, notes.
5. **Submit region.**

## 6. Actions

**`[CAPTURE]` submit button label**
- Enabled when: `[CAPTURE]` always, or only when at least one role is checked and the form validates.
- Does: calls `record_volunteer_signup()` with the volunteer's details, the notes, and the checked role ids. The function resolves or creates the person, writes the signup and its role rows, and increments `quantity_interested` by one per role under a row lock.
- Transaction boundary: the entire function, one transaction.
- Emails queued: `donor_volunteer_confirmation` to the volunteer, including the organization's contact information, and `org_new_volunteer` to the request contact.
- On success: the confirmation in section 8.
- On failure: see section 12. Every entered value retained, including the notes text.

## 9. Empty states

An active request with no roles should not exist, since submission at MP-11 requires at least one. If reached, render the request detail with a stated message rather than an empty roles region and a live submit.

## 10. Mobile differences

`[CAPTURE]`. Capture whether each role becomes a stacked card, where the checkbox sits within it, and whether the notes textarea changes size. A notes field that renders as two visible lines on a phone gets used differently than one that renders as six.

## 11. Authorization

None to view. The response must carry only the fields in section 3 and must not serialize the request's contact person, the organization's phone, or any existing signup data.

`:id` resolving to a non-active request returns the same response as a nonexistent one.

Public write endpoint: rate limited by IP with a per-endpoint ceiling (D10), server-side validated. Every role id is re-validated inside the function against a row lock regardless of what the client sent. The disabled checkbox is a courtesy, never a control.

## 12. Error paths

| Failure | Rendered result |
|---|---|
| `role_full` raised: the last spot was taken while this visitor was filling out the form | The whole transaction rolled back, nothing recorded. Re-render current availability, keep every entered value including the notes, and say plainly which role filled up. If other checked roles are still open, say so, because the visitor can resubmit for those |
| `request_not_active` raised | Tell the visitor the opportunity has closed and link back to PB-03 |
| `role_not_in_request` raised | Tampered or stale payload. Generic failure, logged |
| No roles checked | Blocked at the surface with a message asking them to select at least one |
| Email dispatch fails after a successful signup | The signup stands. Logged as failed, surfaced in ADMIN-06. The volunteer sees success. The organization still sees them at MP-13 |
| A submitted email matches an existing person | Not an error. The person's name is updated to the values just submitted and the signup is attached to the existing row |
| A submitted phone matches a different existing person | Not an error and not a block. The new person is created and flagged for review. The supporter sees nothing unusual |

Losing a volunteer's typed notes on a failed submission is the specific thing to avoid here. Someone who wrote three sentences about their availability and an accommodation they need will not retype it.

## 13. Out of scope for this surface

- Any direct write to a counter column.
- Any login, account, or saved-volunteer behavior.
- Scheduling, shift selection, or calendar integration.
- Confirming a volunteer. That is the organization's action at MP-12.
- Withdrawing interest after submission. No path exists and none is added this week; the confirmation email carries the organization's contact details for exactly this.

## 14. Acceptance

- The surface renders the correct organization's mission, website, logo, and city, resolved through `org_id`.
- An organization field with no value is hidden, not rendered blank.
- A role at zero remaining has a disabled checkbox.
- A submission calls `record_volunteer_signup()` and no code path here updates a counter directly.
- `quantity_interested` increments by exactly one per checked role.
- Two simultaneous signups for the last spot produce one success and one handled `role_full`, with no counter drift. Test deliberately.
- After a failed signup, every entered value including the notes text is still in the form.
- The notes text reaches `volunteer_signups.notes` intact and appears at MP-13.
- Both emails send with resolved names, and the volunteer confirmation carries the organization's contact details and the captured follow-up window.
- A returning person with the same email creates no duplicate person, including someone who previously pledged an item.
- First and last name land in two separate columns, never concatenated.
- A single-word name in either field succeeds.
- A non-active `:id` is indistinguishable from a nonexistent one.
- Desktop and mobile match their screenshots.
