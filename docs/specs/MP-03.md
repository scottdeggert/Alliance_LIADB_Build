## 1. Purpose

Where a representative of an organization registers it with the program. One submission creates the organization at `pending`, creates the primary contact as a person and a user, creates an owner membership at `pending`, and notifies staff. Nothing is public until staff approve at ADMIN-01.

## 2. Entry and exit

**Arrives from:** the public site, and from MP-01 if a registration link exists there.

**Leaves to:** `[CAPTURE]` the post-submit destination. A confirmation state on the same surface and a redirect are both plausible; capture which.

## 3. Data

**Reads:** `populations` (active rows, for the selector).

**Writes, in one transaction:**
- `organizations`, at `status = 'pending'`, with a generated `slug`
- `people`, for the primary contact
- `users`, linked to that person, at `status = 'invited'`
- `org_memberships`, `role = 'owner'`, `status = 'pending'`
- `organizations.primary_contact_person_id`, pointing at the created person
- `organization_populations`, one row per selected population
- `approval_events`, `to_status = 'pending'`, null actor
- `email_log`, for the staff notification

**Functions called:** None.

**Never touches:** `approved_at`, `approved_by`, or any status beyond the initial `pending`. Approval is ADMIN-01's job.

## 4. Layout regions

1. **Page heading and instructional copy.**
2. **Organization details.** Name, website, mission, populations, logo, address, phone.
3. **Primary contact details.** First name, last name, email, phone.
4. **Submit region.**

`[CAPTURE]` confirm the region order and whether the form is one continuous page or a stepped flow.

## 6. Actions

**`[CAPTURE]` submit button label**
- Enabled when: all required fields pass validation.
- Does: the full write in section 3, as one transaction.
- Transaction boundary: every write succeeds or none do. A half-created organization with no contact, or a contact with no organization, is the exact failure this structure exists to prevent.
- Emails queued: `staff_new_org` to both staff addresses. No email to the submitter at this stage.
- On success: the confirmation in section 8.
- On failure: nothing written, the form retains every value including uploads, and the error in section 8 renders.

## 7. Conditional behavior

| Trigger | Result |
|---|---|
| Other selected in populations | The free-text field appears and is `[CAPTURE]` required or optional |
| Other not selected | The free-text field is hidden and `populations_other` is left null |
| Submitted email already exists in `people` | Attach the existing person rather than creating a duplicate. See section 12 |
| Organization name already exists | Blocked with the error in section 8 |

## 11. Authorization

None. Public by design, since organizations self-register.

Because this endpoint is public and writes to the database, it is rate limited by IP with a per-endpoint ceiling (D10), and it needs the same server-side validation as any authenticated mutation. Builder picks the mechanism; the requirement is not optional.

## 12. Error paths

| Failure | Rendered result |
|---|---|
| Email already belongs to an existing person | Not an error. Attach the existing `people` row, create the user and owner membership against it, and proceed. A person who donated last year and now registers an organization is one human and one row |
| Email already belongs to a person with an active owner membership elsewhere | Still proceeds. Multiple memberships are valid; see MP-02 |
| Organization name collides | Blocked, with a message telling the submitter their organization may already be registered and to contact staff. Do not silently append a suffix |
| Address entered without a resolvable city | `city` is required for every approved organization, because public browse renders it as the location. Block at entry rather than letting staff discover it at approval |
| Logo upload fails | The rest of the form still submits. A missing logo is not a blocking condition |
| Transaction fails partway | Nothing written. Every entered value retained |

## 13. Out of scope for this surface

- Approving the organization. ADMIN-01.
- Inviting additional members. MP-06, after approval.
- Editing organization details after submission. MP-05, and see its open question.
- Sending the welcome email. That fires on approval, not on submission.

## 14. Acceptance

- A complete submission creates exactly one organization, one person, one user, one owner membership, one approval event, and one staff notification.
- Submitting with an email that already exists creates no duplicate person.
- First and last name land in two separate columns, never concatenated.
- A submission with a single-word name in either name field succeeds.
- The organization does not appear on any public surface until approved.
- A failed submission writes nothing and loses nothing the user typed.
- `city` is populated for every submission.
- Field order, labels, and copy match the desktop screenshot. Mobile matches the mobile screenshot.
